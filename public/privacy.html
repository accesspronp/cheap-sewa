Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

CheapSewa.Store sells digital subscriptions in Nepal. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password, when you create an account. Passwords are stored in scrambled form (hashed) and cannot be read by us.
  • Order details: the products and plans you order, your phone number, and the amount paid.
  • Payment screenshots: the screenshot you upload to prove payment. Only our admin team can view it.
  • Reviews: any rating or comment you post on a product.

2. How we use it

  • To process and deliver your order.
  • To check your payment and contact you about your order, on WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • To reset your password when you ask us on WhatsApp.
  • To keep the store working and to improve it.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only when needed to complete your order, such as with the digital service provider you bought from, or when the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

Payment screenshots are deleted automatically after 60 days. Order and account details are kept while your account is active and for as long as needed for our records.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information. Message us on WhatsApp or email and we will reply.
